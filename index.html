Teams stay together until one reaches your chosen target with a lead. Both winning players earn one personal win, then the resting player rotates in and game points reset. A tie at the target means another hand.

Normal hands share 100 base points. Tichu is ±100; Grand Tichu is ±200. Choose double victory for 200–0, then add any Tichu adjustments.
